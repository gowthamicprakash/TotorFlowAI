**An AI-powered business management assistant for teachers and educational academies.**

TutorFlow AI is an intelligent assistant designed to help teachers and academy owners manage their teaching businesses through natural voice interactions and an intuitive dashboard.

Instead of navigating multiple screens and manually managing everyday administrative activities, teachers can interact with an AI voice assistant to manage their business more efficiently.

---

## 🚀 Project Vision

Our vision is to simplify the day-to-day operations of teaching businesses by bringing AI-powered assistance into a single application.

TutorFlow AI aims to become a smart business management companion for independent teachers, tutors, and educational academies.

The platform focuses on making business management accessible through natural conversations, reducing repetitive administrative work and allowing teachers to focus more on teaching.

## 🎯 MVP Scope

The initial MVP focuses on two primary components:

### 1. AI Voice Assistant

An integrated AI assistant that allows teachers to interact with the application using natural voice conversations.

Key objectives:

- Voice-based interaction with the AI assistant.
- Understand teacher instructions in natural language.
- Help teachers manage their business operations.
- Provide conversational responses.
- Make everyday business management easier through AI.

### 2. Teacher Dashboard

A centralized dashboard that provides teachers with an overview of their business.

Key objectives:

- View important business information.
- Access and manage available business features.
- Navigate between the dashboard and AI assistant.
- View relevant business information in an organized interface.

### Application Navigation

The application will provide two primary experiences:

- **AI Assistant:** The primary interaction interface after login.
- **Dashboard:** Accessible through a dedicated navigation icon.

Teachers can easily switch between the AI assistant and dashboard.

## 👤 User Onboarding

The initial registration process will collect essential information about the teaching business.

**Required registration information:**
- Business / Academy Name
- Teaching Category (Tuition, Dance, Music, Yoga, etc.)

Additional business information can be collected and updated after registration.

Examples:
- Branch details
- Branch locations
- Contact information
- Other business-related details

This approach keeps the initial registration process simple and easy.

## 🌍 Language Support

**Initial MVP language:** English.

The first version will focus on delivering a complete English-language experience across the application and AI assistant.

Support for additional languages and multilingual conversations can be introduced in future versions.

## 🏗️ Product Architecture

TutorFlow AI is planned as a standalone web application with an integrated AI assistant.

The application will contain:

- User Registration and Authentication
- AI Voice Assistant Interface
- Teacher Dashboard
- Business Information Management
- AI-powered Business Operations

The AI assistant will be accessible directly within the application. No separate Telegram bot is required.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

The technology stack will be finalized during the implementation phase.

The architecture will be designed with scalability, maintainability, security, and future feature expansion in mind.
